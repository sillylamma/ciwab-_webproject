ABOUT US

Centre for Innovation in Water and Agricultural Development (CIWAB)

The Centre for Innovation in Water and Agricultural Development (CIWAB) is a multidisciplinary research center based at Egerton University, Kenya. CIWAB focuses on innovative solutions for water and agricultural challenges in the region, promoting sustainable development through research, training, and community engagement.Established in 2017 as knowledge exchange centre under the Divison of Research and Extension , Egerton University and affliated to the department of Agricultural Egineering, Faculty of Engineering and Technology.Supported by the Netherlands Embassy through the MAU MARA Serengeti Project(MaMaSe).CIWAB focuses on knowledge developpment and dissemination with emphasis on water and ecosystem sustainability.To achieve this CIWAB undertakes applied research and outreach programmes through trainings and capacity building of policy makers, practitioners and end users of natural resources especially water resources.

Vision

To be a leading centre of excellence in innovation for sustainable water and agricultural development in Africa.

Mission

To conduct innovative research, provide training, and promote sustainable practices in water and agricultural development to enhance livelihoods and environmental stewardship in Africa.

Core Values

  • Innovation: We embrace creativity and novel approaches to address water and agricultural challenges.
  • Sustainability: We are committed to promoting practices that ensure long-term environmental health and resource availability.
  • Collaboration: We believe in the power of partnerships and teamwork to achieve our goals.
  • Excellence: We strive for the highest standards in our research, training, and community engagement.
  • Integrity: We uphold honesty, transparency, and ethical conduct in all our activities

Objectives

  • To conduct cutting-edge research on water and agricultural development issues in Africa.
  • To provide training and capacity-building programs for students, professionals, and communities.
  • To develop and promote innovative solutions for sustainable water and agricultural practices.
  • To engage with stakeholders, including policymakers, NGOs, and local communities, to foster collaboration and knowledge exchange.
  • To disseminate research findings and best practices through publications, workshops, and conferences.
