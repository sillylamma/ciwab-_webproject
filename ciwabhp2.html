Commitment

Our Vision

We work to ensure every community has access to safe and sustainable water resources. We envision a future where water scarcity is managed through innovation.

Our Mission

To generate, curate, and share robust data, information, and tools that enhance water security; support diversified livelihoods; and drive economic growth.

Our Purpose

CIWAB aims to enhance understanding of water resources and catchment dynamics. We enable policy makers, practitioners, and communities to make informed decisions for sustainable water use and equitable investments.

Our Objectives

Strategic goals driving our impact.

Knowledge Creation

Conducting interdisciplinary research in water governance and catchment protection.

Capacity Building

Providing training and tools for policy makers, institutions, and communities.

Policy Support

Informing water policy frameworks by supplying evidence and engaging stakeholders.

Innovation

Promoting technologies like GIS and remote sensing for efficient water planning.

Sustainability

Protecting watershed ecosystems to ensure healthy water cycles and biodiversity.

Partnerships

Building collective action among academia, government, and private sectors.
