Frequently Asked Questions

How can I partner with CIWAB?
We welcome partnerships from academic institutions, NGOs, and the private sector. Please reach out via our contact page.
What specific courses do you offer?
We offer short courses on GIS, Water Governance, Integrated Basin Management, and Tractor Operations.
Where is CIWAB located?
We are located at Egerton University, Njoro Campus, within the Faculty of Engineering and Technology.
How can community members get involved?
Community members can participate through our catchment rehabilitation programs and water user associations (WRUAs).
Do you offer internships?
Yes, we offer limited internship slots for students in water engineering and environmental science.
