Centre for Integrated Water and Basin Management – Latest Developments

Building Sustainable Water Futures

News, projects, partnerships, and impact updates from CIWAB.

CIWAB Secures Funding for Water Management & Curriculum Development

Published: 2024

CIWAB has attracted new funding to develop advanced water management projects and improve academic curricula that integrate modern water security principles. The initiative strengthens research capacity and supports proposal development within the centre.

WWF-Kenya Donates Conference & Online Meeting Facilities

Published: 2022

Through its Catchment-to-Tap (C2T) project, WWF-Kenya provided CIWAB with conferencing and online equipment to enhance training and digital collaboration. The equipment will support university-wide events and capacity-building forums.

Short Courses Launched to Strengthen National Water Resource Skills

Published: 2023

CIWAB now offers online short courses including GIS for Resource Mapping, Integrated Water Resources Management, and Remote Sensing. These courses target policymakers, managers, researchers, and practitioners in the water sector.

CIWAB Joins WWF’s “Journey of Water” Campaign

Published: 2024

CIWAB participated in the River Kibos “Journey of Water” campaign, raising public awareness on catchment protection and sustainable basin management.

New Research & Training Complex Completed

Published: 2023

Egerton University completed a new complex dedicated to water research, innovation, and training. The facility strengthens CIWAB’s capacity as a hub for integrated basin management in Kenya.

CIWAB Featured in KEWASNET 2023 Annual Report

Published: 2023

The KEWASNET annual report highlights CIWAB as a contributor to national water governance efforts and civil society coordination.
