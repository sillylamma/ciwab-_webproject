Research
Overview

CIWAB’s research agenda focuses on understanding and addressing water security challenges across catchments. Our interdisciplinary approach integrates hydrology, data systems, governance, ecology, socio-economics, and technology-driven innovation to produce impactful knowledge for communities, institutions, and policy makers.

Key Research Areas

Click on cards to learn more

Water Governance

Strengthening policies, institutional frameworks and accountability systems for equitable and sustainable water use.

Catchment Restoration

Research on watershed rehabilitation, soil conservation, riparian restoration, and sustainable landscape management.

Water Data & Monitoring

Development of hydrological monitoring tools, GIS systems, and digital platforms for data-driven decisions.

Economic Water Use

Evaluating water-use efficiency, economic impacts, valuation, and investment opportunities across sectors.

Livelihood Diversification

Supporting communities to build resilient, water-based livelihoods adaptive to climate and market challenges.

Water-Use Efficiency

Promoting technologies and practices that enable water savings in agriculture, households, and industries.

Ongoing Research Projects

C2T – Catchment to Tap Project

A multi-partner initiative enhancing governance, water safety planning, digital monitoring, and ecosystem protection across water value chains from source to consumer.

Women in Mechanized Agriculture

Investigating gender inclusion in mechanized agriculture and the link between tractor training, productivity, and water use efficiency.

Water Curriculum Innovation

Developing modern curricula for water professionals, integrating digital tools, emerging water challenges, and practical field skills.
